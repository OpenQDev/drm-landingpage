# User settings

![User settings landingpage](/help/user-settings-landingpage.png)

The user page primarily serves to invite new team members via email. Once a member is added, they receive an invitation via email. Closed beta participants do not need to whitelist their colleagues; they will automatically receive access via email. Therefore, currently, only the person who creates the team needs to be whitelisted via GitHub.

If you haven't received our email, please check your spam folder. The official email will be sent from openq@openqmail.dev
