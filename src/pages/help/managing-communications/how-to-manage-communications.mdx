# How to manage your communications

![How to manage communications](/help/communications-create-notes.png)

The notes logging feature allows DevRel representatives to record communication with developers, accessible for both contributors and repositories.

## Functionality:

1. **Recording Communication**: DevRel representatives log notes about discussions, meetings, or any communication with developers.

2. **Centralized Repository**: All notes are stored centrally, enabling access to your team.

3. **Review and Planning**: DevRel reviews notes periodically to understand past interactions, identify themes, and plan future engagements.

4. **Future Planning**: Adding integrations like HubSpot and Salesforce, or integrating services like Gmail and Microsoft 365.
