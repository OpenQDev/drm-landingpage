# What are campaigns?

![Campaign page](/help/campaign-page.png)

A campaign is a list of GitHub Organizations, Repositories, and Users you want to monitor. This feature allows you to create subgroups within your ecosystem, aiding in the identification of growth strategies and assessing the ROI of events such as meetups, conferences, or hackathons by tracking specific contacts. To learn how to create a campaign and explore the various modes available, visit the [creating a campaign page](/help/managing-your-data/campaigns/creating-a-campaign).
