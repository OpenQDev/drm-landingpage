# Fork discovery

![General settings landingpage](/help/fork-discovery.png)

Our fork discovery feature simplifies the process of exploring repositories on GitHub that are derived from a specific parent repository. It allows users to easily identify all repositories that have been forked from a particular repository, providing insights into the network of projects related to the original source.

This feature leverages GitHub's extensive data on repository relationships, enabling users to quickly discover forks and their associated projects. By querying GitHub's database, our system retrieves information about all repositories that have been forked from the specified parent repository, presenting users with a comprehensive list of related projects.

Users can use this feature to gain a deeper understanding of the impact and reach of their projects, identify potential contributors or collaborators, and track the evolution of forks over time. Additionally, it facilitates research and analysis by providing insights into the community and ecosystem surrounding a specific codebase.

Overall, our fork discovery feature enhances the user experience on GitHub by streamlining the process of exploring and understanding the network of repositories connected to a particular project, fostering collaboration and knowledge sharing within the developer community.
