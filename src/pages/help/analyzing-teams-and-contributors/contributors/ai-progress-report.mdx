# Contributor Progress Report

![Ai report](/help/contributor-progress-report.png)

Progress reports entail summaries of commit messages that were made by contributors in the past weeks. These reports are valuable for understanding the recent activities of developers and identifying any commits relevant to your technology. We are continually enhancing this feature and currently exploring methods to provide message templates, enabling you to craft more engaging interactions with developers.

## How to create a report

![Ai report how to](/help/ai-report-how.png)

To generate a report, you can either navigate to the card view of a contributor and select "Changes," then "Summary" or visit the dedicated page of the contributor within the report section.
