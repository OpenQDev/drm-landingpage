# Navigating Your Workspace

After completing the dependency tracking for a list and evaluating all your contacts, you can visit the team and contributor page to review individual contact results. Moreover, our dashboard presents an overview of the total list results, aiding in the formulation of effective OKRs for gauging the impact of each event.

### Utilizing the Sidebar

**List Menu**

The sidebar allows you to navigate between your existing lists or to create new ones. A special section in the sidebar displays the number of contacts tracked in each list, providing options to manage or delete lists. We're also working on introducing a feature for direct list comparisons to help you determine which events yield the best outcomes relative to the budget spent.

**Overview**

The Overview page compiles data from all GitHub organizations, repositories, and contributors, offering a holistic view of your community. We aim to enhance this with a dedicated dashboard and stats page for more detailed tracking, mirroring the functionality available in list monitoring.

**Settings**

The Settings section is where you can add colleagues to your team under the 'Users' category. Invites can be done via email or by sending a link to your team member. Each link is only for one individual.
