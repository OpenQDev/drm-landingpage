# Navigating Your Workspace

After completing the dependency tracking for a campaign and evaluating all your contacts, you can visit the team and contributor page to review individual contact results. Moreover, our dashboard presents an overview of the total campaign results, aiding in the formulation of effective OKRs for gauging the impact of each event.

### Utilizing the Sidebar

**Campaigns Menu**

The sidebar allows you to navigate between your existing campaigns or to create new ones. A special section in the sidebar displays the number of contacts tracked in each campaign, providing options to manage or delete campaigns. We're also working on introducing a feature for direct campaign comparisons to help you determine which events yield the best outcomes relative to the budget spent.

**Overview**

The Overview page compiles data from all GitHub organizations, repositories, and contributors, offering a holistic view of your community. We aim to enhance this with a dedicated dashboard and stats page for more detailed tracking, mirroring the functionality available in campaign monitoring.

**Settings**y

The Settings section is where you can add colleagues to your team under the 'Users' category.
